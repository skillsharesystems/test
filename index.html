About us

Ending Injustice Together

Who we are

Collective action for extraordinary change.

Skillshare Lesotho is a registered local volunteering and development organization working to reduce poverty, injustice and inequality across Lesotho. We believe that collective action by ordinary people, driven by courage, compassion and determination, can bring about extraordinary change.

We work through partnerships with grassroots organizations, use local and international volunteers to build skills and capacity, and harness the power of sport to empower marginalized people. Our overarching goal is to help communities take charge of their own development.

Vision

A just, equitable and empowered society.

Mission

To reduce injustice and inequality by cultivating sustainable livelihoods, upholding human rights, and advancing access to education and health services, including Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR), for the most marginalized and vulnerable communities in Africa.

Our history

Six decades of volunteering in Lesotho.

  1. 1966

    Our roots begin shortly after Lesotho's independence, as International Voluntary Service (IVS).

  2. 1990s

    Our long-term volunteering program leads to the establishment of Skillshare Africa.

  3. 2000

    Skillshare Africa merges with Action Health to form Skillshare International, headquartered in Leicester, UK.

  4. 2001

    Skillshare Lesotho is established as a country program, inaugurated by His Majesty King Letsie III.

  5. 2004

    Archbishop Desmond Tutu praised our work in harnessing the power of ordinary people.

  6. May 2016

    Skillshare Lesotho becomes a locally registered entity after Skillshare International closes operations globally.

2,000+

Over 2,000 development workers and volunteers have been placed in Southern Africa in the last 25 years.

Our goals

Strategic objectives 2022–2026.

  1. 1

    Equip marginalized people with vocational skills, sustainable microenterprises and better employment opportunities.

  2. 2

    Raise awareness of human rights, support human rights institutions, and engage communities in promoting and protecting human rights.

  3. 3

    Empower teachers, schools and communities, and promote equitable access to education for marginalized communities.

  4. 4

    Improve access to health services through training and awareness, with a focus on SRHR, behavior change, and HIV and TB services.

  5. 5

    Maintain a robust monitoring and evaluation system to measure our impact.

  6. 6

    Conduct research and develop innovative solutions to emerging challenges.

Health

Healthcare Accessibility. This area also covers nutrition and hygiene.

Key activities

What we do.

  • SRHR and comprehensive sexuality education for adolescents and young people

Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

Skillshare Lesotho ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our programs, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with Lesotho's Data Protection Act, 2011 and, where they apply, the data protection rules of our partners and funders.

1. Who we are

Skillshare Lesotho is a registered local volunteering and development organization in Lesotho, at 42B Old Europa, United Nations Road, Maseru, Lesotho (P.O. Box 269, Maseru). We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number and message when you contact us, send feedback, subscribe, volunteer, apply for a vacancy or respond to a tender or request for quotations.
  • Donation information: your name, email, country, amount, currency, designation, payment method and, for mobile money, your phone number.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by our payment providers. Card and bank details are not stored on our systems.
  • Program information: to deliver services, we may record details about the people we serve, such as age, district, health service referrals or protection needs. Some of this is sensitive information (see section 5).
  • Website analytics: with your consent, we use Google Analytics cookies to understand how the website is used. See our Cookie Policy.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and feedback, and keep in touch with partners, volunteers and supporters.
  • To process donations and send receipts.
  • To deliver, monitor and evaluate our programs, and to report to funders.
  • To manage recruitment and procurement fairly.
  • To meet our legal, financial and safeguarding obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when needed with payment providers; service providers who host our website, email and data systems; funders and partners, usually as anonymised or combined figures; health, social and protection services when we refer someone, with their consent or where the law or a child's safety requires it; and authorities when required by law.

5. Sensitive information and safeguarding

Information about health (including HIV status), sexual and reproductive health, gender-based violence, migration status or trafficking is handled with strict confidentiality and is only accessed by staff and volunteers who need it. Photos of refugees and asylum seekers are published only with faces blurred or with no identifiable faces.

6. Children

Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed 21 October 2026

Skillshare Lesotho wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people with disabilities. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we do

  • All pages work with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • Images have text alternatives, and every photo has a caption.
  • Text has strong contrast, including text placed over photos.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and to zoom up to 200%.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off.
  • Videos have player controls and never play with sound on their own.

Known limitations

  • The 3D globe on the homepage is decorative. The same information is given in text in the Partners and Where We Work sections.
  • Some partner logos and photos are still being added. Until then, partner names are shown as text.
  • Videos are hosted on YouTube, which provides captions where available.
